Therapeutic Architecture

The Five Disciplines of Athmease Care.

Derived from our physical signboard: Naturopathy, Acupuncture, Nutrition, Ozone, and Yoga. Every therapy is designed to stimulate innate homeostatic mechanisms, improve circulation, and restore systemic balance under physician guidance.

01 / Nature Cure System

Naturopathy

Naturopathy at Athmease is a non-invasive medical discipline recognizing the human body’s self-cleansing and self-regenerating capacity. Rather than relying on synthetic pharmacology, treatments utilize physical modalities—hydrotherapy (water applications at calibrated temperatures), therapeutic mud applications, natural packs, and heliotherapy—to stimulate circulation and facilitate the elimination of metabolic waste.

Each naturopathic regimen is prescribed following an in-depth clinical consultation by Dr. Shirly Annes.S, tailored to the patient’s vitality, thermal responsiveness, and digestive capacity.

  • Hydrotherapy: Calibrated hot and cold water applications to stimulate autonomic microcirculation and lymphatic drainage.
  • Botanical & Clay Packs: Locally prepared mineral mud and cooling abdominal compresses for soothing visceral heat and inflammation.
  • Physiological Elimination: Gentle detoxification protocols supporting hepatic, renal, cutaneous, and bowel elimination channels.

02 / Meridian Medicine

Advanced Acupuncture

Practiced with advanced fellowship credentials, our acupuncture protocols involve the precise insertion of sterile, hair-thin, single-use filiform needles into specific neuro-vascular acupoints along anatomical meridians. This gentle stimulation regulates the central and peripheral nervous systems, modulates local endorphin release, and balances physiological Qi flow.

Dr. Shirly Annes.S specializes in advanced needle technique, ensuring minimal discomfort and profound restorative relaxation during every session. Acupuncture is particularly effective for managing neuromusculoskeletal tension, chronic joint stiffness, digestive irregularities, sleep disturbances, and migraine headaches.

  • Sterile Filiform Needles: Single-use, hair-thin surgical stainless steel needles ensuring absolute clinical safety and comfort.
  • Meridian & Trigger Point Precision: Targeted point protocols addressing systemic imbalances rather than isolated symptoms.
  • Autonomic Relaxation: Inducing parasympathetic dominance to lower chronic muscle guarding and systemic stress tension.

03 / Food as Medicine

Clinical & Naturopathic Nutrition

Dietary guidance at Athmease is rooted in the principle that pure, unadulterated food serves as the foundation for physiological repair. Rather than imposing rigid, unsustainable commercial diets, Dr. Shirly Annes crafts individualized nutritional protocols that honor indigenous South Indian seasonal foods, whole grains, raw enzymes, and medicinal botanicals.

Patients learn how specific dietary timing, digestive rest (intermittent fasting regimens where appropriate), and anti-inflammatory whole foods rebuild the gut microbiome, optimize metabolic stamina, and alleviate systemic sluggishness.

  • Constitutional Dietary Analysis: Custom meal architectures mapped to personal metabolic rate and digestive fire (Agni).
  • Herbal Decoctions & Infusions: Targeted botanical preparations such as moringa, tulsi, turmeric, and cumin to optimize gut function.
  • Therapeutic Juice & Fasting Regimens: Supervised physiological resting periods to assist cellular autophagy and liver recuperation.

04 / Oxygen Therapies

Clinical Medical Ozone

Ozone therapy represents a recognized non-pharmacological modality wherein calibrated concentrations of medical-grade ozone (a triatomic oxygen molecule, O₃) are administered under physician oversight. In a clinical nature cure framework, ozone works as a hormetic biological catalyst—stimulating antioxidant enzyme cascades, enhancing red blood cell oxygen release, and modulating tissue microcirculation.

All ozone applications at Athmease adhere to strict medical dosing guidelines and sterile clinical equipment protocols, ensuring complete patient safety and physiological comfort.

  • Cellular Oxygen Utilization: Enhancing ATP synthesis and cellular respiration in fatigued or congested tissues.
  • Microcirculation Enhancement: Improving rheological properties of blood flow to peripheral vascular beds.
  • Antioxidant Upregulation: Mild controlled oxidative stimulus activating glutathione and superoxide dismutase defense systems.

05 / Yogic Sciences

Therapeutic Yoga & Pranayama

As a core discipline within the BNYS framework, Yoga at Athmease is applied strictly as clinical restorative medicine rather than generic fitness. Under Dr. Shirly Annes's direction, patients receive tailored postural alignments (Asanas) designed to decompress spinal nerve roots, improve joint mobility, and release postural compensation patterns.

Crucially, structured breath regulation (Pranayama) and conscious relaxation routines (Yoga Nidra) are integrated to calm excessive sympathetic overdrive, stabilize blood pressure response, and foster neuro-muscular resilience.

  • Restorative Postural Alignment: Gentle, supported asanas targeting chronic cervical, lumbar, and pelvic biomechanical imbalances.
  • Clinical Pranayama: Rhythmic diaphragmatic breath training to downregulate stress hormones and oxygenate tissues.
  • Neuromuscular Integration: Restoring somatic awareness and posture habits that prevent recurrence of physical strain.
